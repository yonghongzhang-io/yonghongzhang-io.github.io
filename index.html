Research Profile

I am a PhD candidate at Universidad Autónoma de Madrid working at the intersection of climate policy evaluation, causal inference, and AI agents for empirical research. My research focuses on carbon markets, environmental policy evaluation, execution-grounded AI benchmarks, and reliable AI systems for empirical social science.
